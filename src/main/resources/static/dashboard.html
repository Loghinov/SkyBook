Help & FAQ
How to search for flights?

Use the Search Flights tab. Enter origin city/airport code, destination, travel date, and number of passengers, then click Search. Results appear instantly below.

How to book a flight?

Find your flight in search results and click Book. Confirm the number of passengers. Your booking will appear in My Trips with PENDING status.

How to pay for a booking?

Go to My Trips, find your PENDING booking, and click Pay. Enter your card number. After payment the status changes to CONFIRMED.

How to cancel a booking?

In My Trips, click Cancel on any PENDING or CONFIRMED booking. Cancellation does not automatically trigger a refund — contact support for refunds.

Support

For any issues with bookings or payments, contact the SkyBook support team. Admin users can process refunds and manage all bookings from the main app.

Is my payment safe?

This is a demo application — no real payments are processed. Card data entered here is for demonstration purposes only and is not stored or charged.
